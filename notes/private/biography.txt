## Dear reader,  

What brings you here?  
This is my small place on the internet where I write my story or dump my thoughts. The perspective with which I see the world around me, have changed over the years, and I believe it will continue to change, until the time of my demise. The way which I present myself to the outerworld does not really reflect the reality. I skip details and make it sound as normal as possible in order to blend with others around me. 


## Origin (2006 - 2011)

I was born in a really really small town in north bengal. The doctor who was in charge of me was a careless piece of sub human, for whom I nearly died. My mother had to spend nearly 3-4 months in several hospitals, due to postpartum complications -- which resulted in me being malnourished, and shrink in size (that's what they said), and I had a 50/50 chance of survival, but somehow I survived. My skin had turned black too. Thanks to the other doctor who saved my life.  
My first home was a 3 roomed tin-shed structure. I still like to think of it as *my home*. I learnt to walk a little later than kids of my age. I was really cute looking, and everyone in my neighbourhood liked me. My mother used to give me pieces of chalk and told me to write all over the floor, whatever I could. I was a weak child, I had to be taken to the doctor almost every month, for one reason or another. My parents are elementary school teachers, I was left alone with my paternal grandma, while they were away. I used to catch cold easily, so they had placed one 200W light bulb below the side of the bed where I used to sleep -- heaters were costly.  

I was admitted to a public school when I was 3 years old. There was a big tree in the playground - which was my favourite place. We used to sit on the floor (matress). I would burst into rage mode if someone else had sat in my place (first row). No, I did not cry openly. I was admitted directly to Lower Kindergarten. In my first exam, I did not perform well at all. I dont remember the specifics of what happended. But I do remember the final exam, where I had got the perfect score -- sounds silly? yes I agree, but for a 3 year old, it was a big achievement. I had a different name back then, but neither the doctors nor my teachers could write the correct spellling, and often used to predict my sex to be a female. So after an affidavit it was changed to a very common name. Life was not smooth where I lived. I never went out to play with others -- there was a small garden (no matter how small) in front of my home, where I used to spend all my time. I used to crush leaves and grass, and tried to paint the stones with green, and oftentimes used pieces of brick to color them red-brown. Funny. I had once found a nearly-dead bee in my garden. Guess what did I do? I dig up a hole and cremated it, and drew a bee on the soil. I had another hole on the ground, it was my small pond. I used to float small (micro) paper boats after it had rained.  

Once, I was told by my parents that we would be going to our village on that day: the great me, jumped in excitement and rolled down the side of our bed, and got stuck there - until my father somehow pulled me out. And it happend again, not out of excitement, but during the night. I rolled down from the bed, and stayed dangling down due to the mosquito net. I still remember the exact scene. The night-light was of blue color, and the dark-sap-green chair was just a few inches away. I didnt have toys to play with, but I had books. Either of my parents would read out a story, maybe half a page, until I would get hooked to the plot, and then tell me to read the rest all by myself.  

Well I spent a bit more than 4 years in that place, until the political party in charge of my State changed. We had to shift from there, because my father used to write for a few left wing magazines, and had received death threats due to that. I am not joking, it did happen. We sold our plot of land, and came to a bigger town (in comparison to where I used to live). It was so rushed, that I had some problems trying to understand what had really happened?  
My new *home* comprised of 3 rooms, rented on monthly basis. The last time I went to check that house out, it looked as if it was unmaintained; it was covered with unwanted trees/plants all around. I was admitted to a new school. It was a small private one, very close to where I lived. 

## First  (2011 - 2016)

I repeated the Lower Kindergarten again that year (2011), because I was not of age yet. (for getting admitted to the first grade, 6 years of age is a requirement). On the first day of my new school, I could not find out which my class was, neither could I ask out anyone (I was shy perhaps). I ended up reaching my class a bit late, but that was okay. Felt like exploring minecraft caves haha. I don't remember much from 2011, and what else had happended with me. All I remember is getting back from school at around 11:30 am, and then watching Handy Manny on Disney Junior / Art Attack on Pogo. The rest of the day: I used to spend drawing or reading small story books (all in bengali).  
In Upper Kindergarten I gained some consciousness. I started watching Doraemon, Pokemon etc. I was still a loner at my school. We did not have a playground, and there were no parks near my home. I lived near the high road. So it was wake up -> school -> home -> cartoon/study/eat -> sleep -> goto: wake up. I used to play games on a Nokia phone. Perhaps some simpler version of Sudoku? I dont really rememeber. I started to play chess around that time. I used to play from both the sides, and it often ended as a draw. 

In my first grade, I was given an assignment to write: what we wish to do when we grow up. I had written about becoming a "Space Scientist". I remember my class teacher's face when she read mine out, I still dont know if it was surprise or sarcas, because everyone wanted to be a doctor or police or whatever.

I still could not make any friends. Everyone seemed to be different, they talked in a different way, had different things to discuss about, while I could not relate to them. I felt like a misfit. I wont deny that I was not jealous of them, I was jealous of people around me, who used to go out to different places, and talk about their shiny new toys.
I never nagged around and demanded something from my parents, but the one time I asked for a remote controlled toy car, my father said that we can not afford it. It was around 300INR. Perhaps that was the first day, I felt sad and had watery eyes. By the turn of events, there was a book fair, and I pleaded to got myself a book on electronics. That was meant for 10+2 standard kids, I still have no idea HOW did I get that idea to get a book on electronics?? I only knew that toys had some green board and motors inside them which work when I add a battery. Am I proud of what my younger version did? I think yes, I am. The first project I tried was with potatotes: naive me did not understand that the electrodes which were used in the diagram were not mere copper wires. First failure. Sweet. This was late decemeber. 
On the night of, I think, 30th December, I thought to myself: 2012 is gone, it's not coming back, neither will 2013, where does time go then?  
I had imagined some brown blue white tunnel, spiraling tunnel, while thinking about all those. How do I remember so much? Because I revisit these moments from time to time in my mind.

Time for second grade. I had already finished learning much of the stuff which would be taught, back in 2013 itself. During that period of time, February I think, my grandma had fallen sick due to some tumour. And therefore we had to go to Bengaluru for her operation.
It took 3 days, we went on a sleeper berth. The experience was not pleasant for me. I remember vividly about a man who stopped the train by pulling a red chain, he was in a great hurry, I still dont know why he did that. I could observe the color of the soil change from dark yellow-green to yellow and eventually red as the train moved like a big snake. I loved looking out through the window.

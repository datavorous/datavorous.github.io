I'm a researcher working on agent reinforcement learning and harnesses for long horizon tasks.

Currently, I'm interning at a neolab where we're trying to accelerate scientific discovery. Previously, I worked at the Language Technologies Research Centre on DPPNets and optimized kernels for speech models.
